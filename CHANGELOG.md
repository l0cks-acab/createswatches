# Changelog

## 0.2.1
- Swatch Applicator: new 3D steampunk model in Create's style (brass body, copper tank and nozzle, pressure gauge, side cog). The selected block shows in a sample window on top.
- Swatch Carousel: raised base with an andesite band, and a raised cog connector on the back in place of the shaft stub.

## 0.2.0
- The Swatch Typewriter is now the Swatch Carousel: a ribbed brass stand with a spinning ring of swatch tiles and a terminal monitor. Tiles show the palette, or the stocked blocks by hue. Old typewriters do not carry over.

## 0.1.0
- Swatch Typewriter: reads the storage it sits on or a tuned logistics network, swatch and theme tabs, export, return, network orders to an address, unpacks delivered packages. Needs rotation.
- Swatch Card: typed palette, opens a typewriter on that palette.
- Swatch Applicator: places palette blocks from a linked typewriter.
